## 0.1.0 - 2026-08-26

### Added

- Initial release. A deliberately small skill in the cursor-team-kit register: one
  deletion test, a four-step workflow, two lists, and guardrails.
- The body is a hybrid of two drafted candidates — the deletion test as the organizing
  principle (candidate C) with the keep/cut lists as its illustrations (candidate B).
  Both are recorded verbatim in `README.md` so a future editor can see which shape was
  chosen and what it was chosen over.
- Guardrails carry two rules the source brief implied rather than stated: honest gaps
  ("not run", "inconclusive", accepted risk) survive the rewrite, since removing one
  turns uncertainty into an implied pass; and the rewrite introduces no claim the diff
  or other verified context does not support.
